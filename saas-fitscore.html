Diagnostic Framework

The Five Connected Dimensions of SaaS Growth

Most SaaS growth issues aren't just "traffic" or "conversion" problems. We evaluate how the 5 critical components of your commercial engine align to create sustainable compounding revenue.

DIMENSION 01

Positioning

How clearly you articulate who you serve, the acute problem you solve, and why qualified buyers should choose you over every alternative.

  • Ideal customer clarity & buyer relevance
  • Differentiated value proposition
  • Outcome vs. feature messaging
DIMENSION 02

Acquisition

How effectively your website captures qualified intent, removes evaluation anxiety, and transitions high-fit visitors into active signups.

  • Conversion structure & CTA visibility
  • Trust signals & proof architecture
  • Low-friction signup journeys
DIMENSION 03

Activation

How clearly the path to first meaningful product value is communicated and guided before the user loses interest or abandons.

  • First-run product experience clarity
  • Time-to-value expectation setting
  • First milestone definition & guides
DIMENSION 04

Monetisation

How transparently your pricing, tiers, and value limits are structured so upgrading feels like an obvious business decision.

  • Tier differentiation & upgrade catalysts
  • Free vs. paid boundary clarity
  • Value justification for mid-market plans
DIMENSION 05

Retention

Signals of ongoing utility, habit formation, expansion pathways, and ecosystem lock-in that safeguard lifetime value.

  • Evidence of recurring workflow value
  • Integrations & ecosystem reach
  • Continuous education & support assets

Three Highest-Priority Growth Constraints

Key bottlenecks currently impeding customer velocity based on public evidence & context:

Critical Constraint 1. Positioning is Feature-Centric Rather Than Buyer Decision-Centric

Your homepage and headline lead with features rather than an acute, urgent business outcome. Buyers understand what your software does mechanically, but cannot articulate why choosing it over competitor alternatives is a no-brainer decision.

High Priority 2. Activation Friction Between Initial Signup and "Aha" Moment

While acquisition flows capture email signups smoothly, onboarding copy sets generic expectations. First-time users are left to self-explore without guided milestone confirmation, leading to drop-off before product value crystallizes.

Medium Priority 3. Pricing Plan Differentiation Lacks Clear Value Ceilings

Free/starter and paid tier limits are primarily based on arbitrary feature access rather than tangible commercial scale triggers. Mid-market buyers lack a compelling economic rationale to upgrade to higher-value plans.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is SaaS FitScore? +

SaaS FitScore is a structured growth intelligence scorecard designed for B2B SaaS founders. It evaluates the commercial readiness of your product across five interconnected dimensions: Positioning, Acquisition, Activation, Monetisation, and Retention, pinpointing where user conversion stalls.

How are the scores calculated? +

We use a deterministic, evidence-based scoring framework. We inspect public website messaging, pricing pages, and signup funnels alongside your founder-supplied business context. Scores are calculated objectively using weighted criteria—not arbitrary AI guesses.

Do you require access to my analytics or private code? +

No. We never ask for passwords, private customer records, or analytics access. The assessment relies exclusively on publicly discoverable website data and the high-level business context you provide in the questionnaire.

Yes. The diagnostic scorecard, evidence breakdown, and 30-60-90 day improvement priorities are 100% free with no obligation.
