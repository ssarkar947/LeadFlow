Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose a bespoke e-commerce website over Shopify for FMCG? −

Bespoke e-commerce websites are custom-engineered from scratch to maximize conversion and speed. Unlike template-dependent systems like Shopify, custom sites load instantly, carry zero platform-percentage fees, and allow complete freedom in building unique checkout funnels, subscription systems, and visual layouts tailored specifically to consumer goods shopping behaviors.

How do custom AI agents drive sales and brand value for FMCG products? +

Our custom AI agents operate on your storefront and consumer channels like WhatsApp. They recommend routines, suggest food recipes, coordinate loyalty coupon payouts, manage distributor orders, and track marketing analytics. By providing instant personalized engagement, they turn cold traffic into loyal, repeat customers.

How does CGI product animation improve digital ad performance? +

CGI allows you to showcase packaging details, fluid physics, and material textures with extreme precision. These hyper-realistic 3D animations capture attention on social media feeds instantly, explaining complex product benefits in seconds, which leads to higher click-through rates (CTR) and lower customer acquisition costs (CAC).

How do influencer marketing and UGC boost retail shelf velocity for FMCG brands? +

While traditional branding builds passive awareness, creator-led UGC and micro-influencer campaigns build immediate active trust. By showing real-world product usage, taste tests, and daily routines, we drive localized, high-intent consumer demand that directly translates to faster inventory turn and higher sales velocity on physical supermarket shelves.

What is the typical timeline and process for a custom CGI campaign or bespoke web build? +

A standard CGI product animation or professional video ad campaign typically takes 2 to 3 weeks from creative strategy and asset modeling to final render. Custom e-commerce web builds range from 4 to 6 weeks, which includes strategic design, conversion funnel architecture, performance coding, and rigorous database speed optimization.
