Privacy Policy

How AdsVise collects, uses, protects, and respects your personal data across our website and advertising campaigns.

1. Overview & Business Information

This Privacy Policy applies to AdsVise ("AdsVise", "we", "us", or "our"), operating at https://adsvise.in. AdsVise is a marketing intelligence startup helping businesses shape customer perception in their favor through strategic positioning, LinkedIn growth, performance advertising, and custom digital infrastructure.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect information obtained from visitors to our website, individuals who fill out lead generation forms (including Meta / Facebook Instant Lead Forms), and clients who engage our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as certain data automatically collected when you interact with our digital channels:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Information: Full Name, Business Email Address, Phone Number, WhatsApp Number.
  • Business Details: Company Name, Company Size, Industry, Website URL, and specific growth objectives or inquiries submitted via forms.
  • Communication Data: Any details, feedback, or messages sent via contact forms, lead capture popups, or email correspondence.

B. Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Lead Generation Ads Data

If you submit your information through a Meta Lead Generation Form (such as Facebook Instant Forms or Instagram Lead Ads), we collect the contact details you voluntarily submit in that form (e.g., Name, Email, Phone Number, Company Name). This data is transmitted securely to AdsVise in full compliance with Meta's Advertising Policies and Meta Lead Ads Terms.

C. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you visit our website, we automatically collect device and browsing statistics through cookies and tracking pixels, including:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, and device parameters.
  • Pages viewed, session duration, click patterns, and referring website URLs.
  • Analytics and conversion events via tools such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel (Facebook Pixel).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Fulfilling Lead Inquiries: Contacting you to respond to your specific request, discuss positioning strategies, schedule consultations, and deliver requested strategy proposals.
  • Business Communication: Sending transactional updates, administrative notifications, and follow-up communications related to your inquiry.
  • Marketing & Insights: Sending relevant marketing intelligence insights, industry briefs, or service announcements (you can opt out at any time).
  • Ad Campaign Optimization: Measuring the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns on platforms like Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and LinkedIn to ensure we reach the right audience.
  • Security & Compliance: Protecting our website against fraud, unauthorized access, or legal violations.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We only share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website, managing customer relationships, or conducting our business, strictly under confidentiality agreements:

  • CRM & Database Providers: Secure cloud infrastructure (such as Supabase, encrypted database storage) to store lead entries.
  • Communication & Email Partners: Professional transactional email providers (e.g., Google Workspace, Nodemailer) to process email notifications.
  • Analytics & Advertising Partners: Google Analytics and Meta Advertising tools to analyze website traffic and measure ad performance.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation to protect our rights or property.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes cookies, web beacons, and tracking pixels (including the Meta Pixel) to enhance your browsing experience and measure campaign performance.

Managing Cookies & Ad Preferences:

  • You can configure your browser settings to decline or clear cookies at any time.
  • To manage your Meta / Facebook ad preferences and opt out of interest-based ads on Meta platforms, visit your Facebook Ad Preferences.
  • To opt out of Google Analytics tracking, visit the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical, organizational, and physical security measures (including SSL/TLS encryption, restricted API access, and secure database protocols) to protect your personal data against unauthorized disclosure, loss, or alteration.

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by applicable legal, tax, or regulatory obligations.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request full deletion of your personal data from our systems.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link or replying to our email.

How to Submit a Data Deletion Request

If you have filled out a form on our website or through a Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Lead Ad and wish to have your data permanently deleted from our records, please email us directly at:

digitaladsexpresso@gmail.com

Please use the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your Name and Email Address. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 48 business hours.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external third-party websites or services (such as LinkedIn, YouTube, or Facebook). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every website you visit.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our business practices, technology, or legal requirements. When updates occur, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website or lead forms following any updates signifies acceptance of the revised policy.
