1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Water Can Manager, a specialized software application and enterprise service engineered by AdsVise ("AdsVise", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy applies to all versions of the Water Can Manager mobile application, web dashboard, APIs, and associated services (collectively, the "Services").

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal data of all users, including water plant owners/vendors, dispatch managers, delivery agents/drivers, and retail/corporate customers who receive water deliveries through our platform.

2. Information We Collect

To provide reliable water distribution, dispatch, and can-deposit tracking, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Information You Provide to Us Directly:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, business/company name, mobile phone number, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Customer Delivery Details: Customer name, delivery address, apartment/floor details, contact phone number, and special delivery notes.
  • Can Inventory & Transaction Records: Number of water cans delivered, empty cans returned, can deposit amounts, daily delivery logs, and payment modes (Cash, UPI transaction reference numbers).
  • Support Communications: Messages, feedback, inquiries, or bug reports submitted to our customer care team.

B. Information Collected Automatically:

  • Device & Log Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, app version, network state, and crash analytics.
  • Usage Analytics: Features accessed, buttons clicked, sync times, and app performance metrics to ensure zero disruption during delivery runs.

3. Device Permissions & Specific Purposes

The Water Can Manager app may request access to certain device features. These permissions are strictly required for operational functionality:

  • Location (Precise GPS & Approximate): Used by delivery agents to view customer delivery addresses on map navigation, calculate optimal multi-stop routes, and verify delivery completion at the customer's doorstep.
  • Camera & Photos/Storage (Optional): Used solely to allow delivery personnel or admins to capture proof of delivery (e.g. signature, can drop photo) or scan QR codes on water cans/delivery slips.
  • Notifications: Used to dispatch real-time order alerts, route assignments, inventory threshold warnings, and customer payment updates.
  • Phone / SMS (Optional): Allows drivers to initiate calls or send automated WhatsApp/SMS delivery confirmation receipts directly to the recipient customer.

Google Play Policy Notice: Background Location Usage

For delivery personnel and driver accounts, Water Can Manager may request background location access during an active, logged-in delivery shift. This background data is used strictly to provide real-time fleet visibility to the distributor dispatch manager and to accurately compute route fuel efficiency. Background tracking is immediately terminated when the driver clocks out or ends the delivery run. We do not track location outside of active delivery duties, and we NEVER sell location data.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for genuine business and operational purposes:

  • To manage customer water subscriptions, generate digital monthly attendance cards, and prevent loss of reusable 20-litre cans.
  • To dispatch daily delivery run-sheets to drivers and optimize driving routes.
  • To provide instant delivery receipts, payment links, and billing summaries via SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent entries, multiple unaccounted can shortages, or unauthorized account access.
  • To maintain app reliability, release security patches, and deliver technical support.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

  • Security Deposit: Vendors may charge a refundable or adjustable security deposit per can. The Water Can Manager app maintains an immutable ledger of active cans at customer premises and deposits collected.
  • Return Obligation: Customers are obligated to return empty, undamaged, food-grade water cans of equivalent quality upon delivery of replacement cans or upon service cancellation.
  • Damaged or Lost Cans: If a customer fails to return an empty can or returns a damaged/punctured/contaminated can, the vendor holds the full right to deduct the replacement value from the customer's security deposit or levy a replacement surcharge.
